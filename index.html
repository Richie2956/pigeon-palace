Found a pigeon?

Do these three things, in this order. The first aid page has the detail and the phone numbers.

  1. WarmthA closed box in a quiet room, with a wrapped hot water bottle at one end.
  2. Then fluidsOnce the bird is warm and can stand, dip the tip of its beak in water with a pinch each of salt and sugar. Never squirt liquid in.
  3. Then foodLast, and only once the bird has passed droppings.

If a cat or dog has had the bird, it needs a vet the same day, even with no wound you can see.

Not sure what is wrong?

Tick the signs you can see. The checker ranks 76 conditions against them, and puts the red flags that mean a vet now at the top.

Tick what you can see

  • Fluffed up, quiet, eyes closing
  • Drinking far more than usual
  • Cold to the touch
  • Pale mouth lining and pale skin
  • Swollen or tight abdomen
  • Green faeces

Use it as an app

Add the course to your phone's home screen and it opens like any other app, with or without a signal.

iPhone

  1. Open the app page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Tap 'Add to Home Screen'.

Android

  1. Open the app page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu.
  3. Tap 'Install app' or 'Add to Home screen'.
